02

Gameplay and local storage

DUNKYARD RIVALS is a single-player Windows game against computer-controlled opponents. It has no online multiplayer, no chat and no game-specific player account.

Settings and career save data are stored locally on the computer. They remain under the player's control. DUNKYARD RIVALS does not transmit these local saves or settings to Andreas Boehler.

A review of the game scripts found no built-in HTTP, WebSocket or other networking functions. This statement describes the reviewed development state; it is not a complete technical certification or a guarantee that the operating system, a store client, security software or other independent components do not make their own connections.

AI tools were used during development for graphics, code and text. This does not cause player data to be sent to AI services while the game is being played.

Andreas Boehler does not receive personal data from the game's purely local storage. Providing personal data to the creator is not required to play. The game does not carry out automated decision-making or profiling on behalf of Andreas Boehler.
